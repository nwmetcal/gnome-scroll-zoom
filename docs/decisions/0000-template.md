# A short title, saying what was decided

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by 00NN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD, the day of the last change
- Decided by: who

## Context

Two or three sentences: the situation and the question, in neutral words.

## Options

- Option one
- Option two

## Decision

Option one, because the reason that mattered most.

### Consequences

- Good: what it makes easy.
- Bad: what it costs or rules out.

### Checked by

The test, scenario, or review that fails when the decision is broken.

## Reversing it

What undoing this would take, and the steps. A decision that is costly to reverse gets a
seam that makes reversing cheaper before it is taken.
